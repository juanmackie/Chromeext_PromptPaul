# Weather Guide

## Terminology
- **Humidity**: The amount of water vapor in the air.
- **Precipitation**: Any product of condensed atmospheric water vapor that falls to the ground.
- **Barometric Pressure**: The pressure exerted by the atmosphere.

## How to read a weather report
1. Check the temperature.
2. Review precipitation chance and type.
3. Note wind speed and direction.
